Sometimes, stepping back doesn’t mean you are giving up.

Sometimes everything just feels too heavy, and you need something that makes your heart feel a little lighter.

Do you necessarily need someone’s words? Sometimes, yes. It can make you feel less sad and less alone, especially when those words come from the person you love.

I’m not trying to be dramatic. Life has just been so rough lately.

Arguments, failures, disappointments, break-ups, family issues.

All at once.

Sometimes I can barely hold everything together.

But crying doesn’t mean you are weak. Feeling exhausted doesn’t mean you are giving up. We are human. We feel pain. We get tired. Sometimes we need to step back because carrying everything at once becomes too much.

I’ve been through worse before.

I lost Grandma, my oldest sister, and Papa in consecutive years. I worked for my tuition while being treated like a low-life creature.

But I survived.

I graduated.

I kept going.

And along the way, I met people who taught me so many things I didn’t know. I’m grateful for those people, especially my one mentor who has given me so much of his time, values, and advice.

He has been patient with me, corrected me, and kept teaching me even when I made it exhausting for him.

And I know I still messed up.

There are things he already taught me that I still fail to apply properly. Sometimes I’m too slow. Sometimes I disappoint him. And that hurts, especially because I know how much he just wants me to learn.

Lately, everything has been hitting me at once, and I’m tired.

So maybe I need to step back sometimes.

Not because I’m quitting.

Not because I’ve stopped trying.

I just need a little space to carry the weight without breaking under all of it.

I’ve suffered too much, lost too much, and survived too much to give up now.

Giving up is still not in my vocabulary.
